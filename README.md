# GenZ Smart Dental Clinic

## GoHighLevel CRM and Automation Project

This project is a complete GoHighLevel CRM and automation system built for GenZ Smart Dental Clinic.

## Project Overview

The system manages the complete patient journey from initial lead capture through appointment booking, follow ups, appointment outcomes, and treatment conversion.

The automation is designed to identify incoming leads, create or update contacts, assign leads to the appropriate team member, manage opportunities, communicate with patients, and track the patient journey through treatment.

## Lead Management Pipeline

New Lead → Contacted → Appointment Booked → Qualified → Treatment Started

### Pipeline Stages

• New Lead: Lead has entered the system

• Contacted: Sales team has contacted the patient

• Appointment Booked: Patient has booked a dental consultation

• Qualified: Patient attended the consultation and is qualified for treatment

• Treatment Started: Patient has started treatment

## Appointment Management

The system handles different appointment outcomes through dedicated automation workflows.

• Appointment booked: Confirmation and scheduled reminders are sent

• Appointment completed: Opportunity moves to the Qualified stage and post consultation follow up is triggered

• Appointment no show: Sales notification and patient follow ups are triggered

• Appointment cancelled: Sales notification and rescheduling communication are triggered

## Treatment Conversion

When an opportunity is converted to a treatment opportunity, the Treatment Conversion workflow sends a confirmation email and continues follow up communication.

The workflow checks whether the opportunity has moved to the Treatment Started stage before sending additional follow ups.
